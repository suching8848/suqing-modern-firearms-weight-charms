[h2]中文[/h2]
[b]男人的梦想就是带一把步枪和一把反器材大狙，但是好重，所以做了这个模组。适合喜欢收集、囤积物资和多带枪械的玩家，会明显影响真实性与负重平衡。[/b]
适用 B42.21–42.22 单人，版本 1.2.2-rc.1。B42.22.2 原生装卸和旧挂件读回已离线通过，游戏内待验收。需要另订阅并启用[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3633421539]ModernFirearmsSystem（MFS）[/url]，让本模组在MFS之后加载。不需要补丁合集或独立库存优化补丁。
[h3]两种挂件[/h3]
[list]
[*]轻型：最多减重1.5，挂件自重0.1。
[*]重型：最多减重5.0，挂件自重0.2；裸枪基础重量必须严格大于5.0，加装重配件不会改变资格。
[/list]
占用枪械Misc杂项槽，每枪只能装一个，与同槽配件互斥，可与普通枪带共存，不占Noir人物背枪位置。受影响枪体及附件最低保留0.1；弹药、弹匣内容正常计重，原生手持和背挂系数保留。它不添加枪上可见模型。
[h3]获得与使用[/h3]
制作菜单“武器”分类搜索“蘇清”或“减重挂件”。轻型需机械1、铝箔2、螺丝2、胶带1用量；重型需机械3、铝箔4、螺丝4、胶带2用量，螺丝刀保留。铝箔是Base.Aluminum，铝废料不能替代。挂件也可在尚未生成物资的枪店、警局或军事武器柜中找到；重型仅加入军事武器柜，不补发旧箱。
挂件放人物主物品栏，确保Misc空闲，再用原生右键或MFS检视界面装卸；安装不额外要求工具，可反复使用。旧版物品ID保留，Misc空闲时迁移。停用前先拆下枪上挂件并处理已有挂件物品。

[h2]English[/h2]
[b]Want to carry a rifle and a large sniper rifle, but find them too heavy? Attach a reusable weight-reduction charm. Made for collectors, stockpilers and players carrying several guns; it substantially changes realism and carry-weight balance.[/b]
For B42.21–42.22 single-player, version 1.2.2-rc.1. Native equip and legacy charm readback passed offline on B42.22.2; gameplay testing is pending. Subscribe to and enable [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3633421539]ModernFirearmsSystem (MFS)[/url] separately, then load this add-on after it. Neither the patch collection nor a separate inventory optimization patch is required.
[h3]Two charms[/h3]
[list]
[*]Light: up to 1.5 weight reduction; charm weight 0.1.
[*]Heavy: up to 5.0 weight reduction; charm weight 0.2. Bare weapon base weight must be strictly above 5.0; heavy attachments do not change eligibility.
[/list]
Uses the firearm's Misc slot, one charm per gun. Excludes other Misc attachments, coexists with ordinary slings and does not occupy Noir character carry positions. Affected gun and attachment weight stays at least 0.1. Ammunition and magazine contents still count normally; native held and hotbar multipliers remain. No visible gun-mounted model is added.
[h3]Obtaining and use[/h3]
Search for SuQing or the charm name in the Weapons crafting category. Light: Mechanics 1, aluminum foil 2, screws 2 and duct tape 1 use. Heavy: Mechanics 3, aluminum foil 4, screws 4 and duct tape 2 uses. The screwdriver is kept. Aluminum means Base.Aluminum, not scrap. Charms can also appear in newly populated gun-store, police or military weapon storage; heavy charms are added only to military storage. Existing filled containers are not backfilled.
Keep the charm in main inventory and Misc empty, then attach or remove it through native right-click actions or MFS inspection. No extra installation tool is required; charms are reusable. Legacy item IDs remain and migrate when Misc is free. Before disabling, detach all installed charms and deal with existing charm items.
[h3]Compatibility and feedback[/h3]
The author reports normal play with their full B42.21 single-player setup. This is not individual validation of every firearm, item or combination. Other ranged weapons with ammunition types may qualify, without a guarantee for every firearm mod. Multiplayer and dedicated servers are untested. Choose either Workshop or manual installation.
