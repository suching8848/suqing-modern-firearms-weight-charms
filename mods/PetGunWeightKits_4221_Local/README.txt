中文
极大影响真实性与平衡性，请谨慎添加。适合喜欢收集、囤积物资和多带枪械的屯屯鼠玩家。
制作缘由：想同时携带步枪和大狙，但负重太高。
Build 42.21单人用途维护；需ModernFirearmsSystem，加载在其后；多人和专用服务器未验证。
前置：https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3633421539
本模组：https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3824003141
Mod ID：Codex_PetGunWeightKits_4221

轻型最多减重1.5、自重0.1；重型最多5.0、自重0.2，裸枪脚本重量须严格大于5.0。
受影响枪体和附件最低0.1，弹药和弹匣内容正常计重；占Misc槽，不能叠加，可共存普通枪带。
制作：轻型机械1、铝箔2/螺丝2/胶带1用量；重型机械3、铝箔4/螺丝4/胶带2用量；均保留螺丝刀。
铝箔为Base.Aluminum。轻型自然掉落于枪店/警局/军事武器柜，重型较稀有地出现于军事武器柜；不补发旧箱。
挂件放主物品栏，Misc留空，使用原生右键或MFS检视界面装卸；安装不额外要工具，可重复使用。
旧Sling版在Misc空闲时迁移，占用时保持原位生效。动态减重在读档/装卸/装备/转移/容器检查时恢复。

手动安装：退出游戏并保留备份，将本文件夹放入用户Zomboid/mods，保留42和common目录，再在目标单人档启用。
工坊和手动版使用同一Mod ID，选择一种来源。停用前先拆下全部挂件，处理已有挂件物品并备份存档。

English
Greatly changes realism and balance; use with care. Intended for collectors, stockpilers and players carrying several firearms.
I made it to carry both a rifle and a large sniper rifle despite their weight.
Maintained for Build 42.21 single-player. Requires ModernFirearmsSystem and loads after it; multiplayer and dedicated servers are untested.
Requirement: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3633421539
This mod: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3824003141
Mod ID: Codex_PetGunWeightKits_4221

Light: up to 1.5 reduction, own weight 0.1. Heavy: up to 5.0, own weight 0.2; bare script weapon weight must be strictly above 5.0.
Affected gun/attachments retain at least 0.1. Ammunition/magazine contents count normally. Uses Misc; one charm per gun; ordinary slings can coexist.
Crafting: light Mechanics 1, aluminum foil 2/screws 2/duct tape 1 use; heavy Mechanics 3, foil 4/screws 4/tape 2 uses. A screwdriver is kept.
Foil means Base.Aluminum. Light charms appear in gun store/police/military gun storage; heavy charms are rarer military loot. No backfilling old containers.
Keep the charm in main inventory and Misc empty. Attach/remove using native right-click or MFS inspection; no extra installation tool; reusable.
Legacy Sling charms migrate when Misc is free, otherwise remain active in place. Dynamic reduction is restored on load/attachment changes/equipping/transfers/container checks.

Manual installation: exit the game, retain a backup, copy this folder into your user's Zomboid/mods, preserve 42/common, then enable in the target single-player save.
Workshop/manual versions share one Mod ID; choose one source. Before disabling, detach all charms, deal with charm items and back up the save.

The author reports normal play with this current collection in their own B42.21 single-player save.
